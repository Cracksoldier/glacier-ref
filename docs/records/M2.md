## Known limitations

- Note heights are computed with the system font. Another platform's font metrics can produce slightly different heights; heights are recomputed on the next edit (M3 may recompute on load).
- Duplicating group members in group-edit mode duplicates the whole group.
- While a note is edited, all window shortcuts are suspended, including future ones such as Save; leave editing first. To be revisited with M3 saving.
- Always on Top remains guidance-only on Wayland (M4.03).
